Adds an Equipment field to Repair Orders, alongside Product to Repair. A
Repair Order must be linked to a Product, an Equipment, or both.

**Table of contents**

.. contents::
   :local:

Usage
=====

On a Repair Order, set the Product to Repair, the Equipment, or both.
The Equipment column is also available in the Repair Orders list view.
